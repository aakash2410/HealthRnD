Intervention → Data Flow → Expected Health Impact
How each AI intervention closes a specific care gap. Impact estimates drawn from published pilots and clinical evidence.
Stage Intervention Gap Closed Data Flow Expected Impact
ANC Garbhini-GA2 (AI gestational age) Naegele inaccuracy; ~30% of women have irregular LMP FHIR DiagnosticReport → HMIS → ABHA Mean GA error: ~9 days → ~3 days; corrects preterm classification for ~30% of cohort (GARBH-Ini, 2024)
ANC ARMMAN mMitra (dropout prediction + IVR) Reactive ASHA follow-up; high dropout at ANC visits 4–8 Risk score → ANM dashboard → automated IVR 15–20% improvement in ANC completion; earlier identification of high-risk pregnancies (ARMMAN RCT)
ANC AI-enabled POCUS (ultrasound) Ultrasound confined to district hospitals; non-specialists lack image acquisition skill FHIR DiagnosticReport → ABHA · anomaly flag to specialist Earlier detection of placenta praevia, malpresentation, anomalies; task-shifts ultrasound to CHC level
Labor Janitri / DotoCare / Sybil / FetalLite (AI CTG) CTG read by variable-skill clinicians; fetal distress identified late from clinical signs FHIR Observation (CTG classification) → real-time obstetrician alert Faster time-to-intervention for pathological CTG; lower intrapartum stillbirth and HIE rates (Janitri UP pilot, 2023)
Labor Gradi + Dozee + Stryker–Titan (PPH detection) Visual blood loss estimation underestimates PPH by 33–50%; delayed uterotonic intervention FHIR Observation (blood loss, EWS) → escalation alert PPH detection 15–30 min earlier; PPH accounts for 25–30% of maternal deaths in India
Postpartum Dozee (2-hr danger window monitoring) Post-delivery vitals checked at intervals; 2-hour window missed in understaffed wards Continuous FHIR Observation (EWS score) → nurse alert Continuous surveillance without added nursing workload; reduces missed secondary PPH and eclampsia
Postpartum Shishu Maapan (AI birth anthropometry) Beam balance + tape measure errors frequent; WHO z-scores rarely computed at birth FHIR Observation (weight, length, z-score) → Child ABHA Accurate LBW identification at birth; triggers KMC and NICU referral; prevents missed wasting at discharge
Neonatal InAccel Saans / Vayu / BabyFlow 100 (CPAP) RDS: delayed CPAP due to device/skill shortage; nurse-to-baby ratio unsustainable in public NICUs FHIR Observation (SpO₂, CPAP params) → Child ABHA · NHCX claim CPAP reduces neonatal RDS mortality 30–50%; Saans supports 1 nurse per 40–50 babies vs standard 1:4
Neonatal IoT-Enabled Incubator Temperature drift undetected; manual parameter charting with gaps between rounds FHIR Observation (temp, humidity, weight) · alarm log to nursing station Prevents hypothermia-related deaths; hypothermia on admission linked to 2–3x higher mortality in preterm infants
Neonatal Dozee (neonatal sepsis AI) Sepsis detected from clinical signs; typically 12–24h after onset FHIR Observation (sepsis risk score) → neonatologist alert Early sepsis flag 6–12h before clinical signs; neonatal sepsis CFR drops from 20–30% to under 10% with early antibiotic initiation
