Why ABDM?
Without ABDM
  • Each startup (CareNX, ARMMAN, InAccel Saans) holds its own siloed patient record — no shared identity to link them
  • AI risk scores (pre-eclampsia, growth-faltering) live inside individual apps; the referring OB specialist cannot see them
  • No standard consent mechanism — mother cannot grant a specialist access to her full ANC history in one action
  • Tele-consult bookings are fragmented across each platform's own channel
  • Maternity claims require separate submissions to each payer portal
What ABDM enables here
  • ABHA provides a single identity that links every device reading, AI output and clinical note to the same mother and child — across all providers
  • HIE-CM lets the mother share her full care record with an OB specialist in two clicks — the "coordinated" in coordinated care is only possible with this consent layer
  • ARMMAN's risk DiagnosticReport and InAccel Saans's NICU Observations become part of the permanent ABHA-linked record, not disposable app data
  • UHI standardises tele-consult booking so any OB-listed on HFR/HPR is discoverable

Why ABDM?
Without ABDM
  • ASHA manually re-enters the same mother's data into three separate systems: RCH portal, U-WIN, and Poshan Tracker — every visit
  • When a mother is referred up to a district hospital, the receiving clinician has no record; she starts from scratch
  • Khushi Baby's NFC records and Shishu Maapan's growth data stay in separate apps with no shared patient ID to link them
  • No portable patient identity means community records are lost if the ASHA changes or the mother moves sub-centre
What ABDM enables here
  • ABHA (offline mode, mobile number as key) gives the mother a portable ID — one capture automatically feeds RCH, U-WIN and Poshan Tracker on sync, eliminating triple entry
  • HIE-CM (deferred) means the district-hospital clinician can pull her full community record the moment she arrives and connectivity is available
  • Child ABHA created at birth ensures the newborn's immunization record in U-WIN is linked from day one — no gap when the family leaves the facility
  • Khushi Baby and Shishu Maapan data become part of the same ABHA-linked record, not isolated app exports

Why ABDM?
Without ABDM
  • Hospital submits maternity pre-auth separately to each insurer/TPA portal — different formats, 4–7 day turnaround per payer
  • At discharge, there is no standard handoff: the newborn's immunization record does not automatically reach the community ASHA or U-WIN
  • Mother has no visibility into claim status; relies on hospital billing staff for updates
  • InAccel Saans NICU data and EkaCare discharge summary are generated by separate apps — no shared patient record to anchor them
  • Hospital cannot claim the Digital Health Incentive without ABHA-linked records
What ABDM enables here
  • NHCX (via ABDM) replaces fragmented payer portals with a single FHIR window — one pre-auth request reaches all 50+ onboarded insurers and PMJAY SHA simultaneously
  • HIE-CM lets the mother consent to share her InAccel Saans NICU record and EkaCare discharge summary with the insurer as claims evidence — reducing fraud documentation delays
  • Child ABHA at birth registration automatically links the newborn's hospital record to U-WIN, closing the facility-to-community immunization gap that today causes dropout
  • ABHA linkage at admission triggers the Digital Health Incentive — a direct financial reason for private hospitals to participate
